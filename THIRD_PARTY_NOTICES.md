# Third-party filter data notices

This package contains generated filtering rules derived from externally maintained lists. The extension itself is independent from those projects and from Ghostery.

- **EasyList** — community advertisement filters. Source: `https://easylist.to/easylist/easylist.txt`. Use and redistribution remain subject to the license published by the EasyList project.
- **AdGuard Tracking Protection Filter** — source: `https://filters.adtidy.org/extension/chromium/filters/3.txt`. Use and redistribution remain subject to AdGuard Filters' published license and attribution terms.
- **AdGuard Annoyances Filter** — source: `https://filters.adtidy.org/extension/chromium/filters/14.txt`. Use and redistribution remain subject to AdGuard Filters' published license and attribution terms.
- **URLHaus Online Malware Filter** — source: `https://malware-filter.gitlab.io/malware-filter/urlhaus-filter-ag-online.txt`; generated from URLHaus data. The upstream project publishes the browser filter under permissive terms and URLHaus data is provided for abuse prevention.

Generated metadata records the exact local or remote source used during a build. Before public distribution, the distributor must review and comply with each current upstream license; this development package is not a legal clearance opinion.
